Straight answers

What does an AI audit for a manufacturer actually cover?

An 82-point diagnostic across six dimensions: business context, technology maturity, automation opportunity, data infrastructure, decision authority, and investment readiness. Built for shops carrying CUI, export-controlled prints, and ISO 9001 quality records, not a generic SaaS checklist.

We are not a defense contractor. Does CMMC or ITAR even apply to us?

Plenty of shops touch CUI or export-controlled data two or three tiers down a supply chain without realizing it. The audit tells you where you actually stand instead of guessing from a prime's flowdown clause.

How is this different from a cybersecurity audit?

A cybersecurity audit looks at your network. This looks at where AI tools touch your data, your floor, and your decisions, then maps that against your CMMC, ITAR, and ISO 9001 exposure specifically.

Do we have to hire you to fix what the audit finds?

No. The audit stands on its own. You get the findings and a prioritized roadmap either way. Some shops run it themselves, some bring us back for the Operational Blueprint build.

How long does the audit take and what does it cost?

Most shops finish in a few weeks, start to finish. It is a fixed-phase, flat-fee engagement, scoped in one call, so you know the number before we start.
