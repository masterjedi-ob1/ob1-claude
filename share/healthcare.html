Questions we actually get from healthcare leaders

Does an AI audit replace our HIPAA risk assessment?

No. It sits next to it. A HIPAA risk assessment tests your security controls. Our audit tests where AI tools touch PHI, who authorized them, and whether a BAA covers the model vendor.

What if a vendor's AI feature was never disclosed to us?

More common than you would think. The audit maps every AI touchpoint in your stack, including features bundled into tools you already pay for, so you can see what needs a BAA and what needs to be turned off.

Do you need access to patient records to run this?

No. The audit reviews systems, contracts, and workflows, not patient data itself. Nothing PHI-bearing crosses into our process.

How is this different from an IT security audit?

IT security asks if your systems are locked down. This asks a narrower question: where is AI operating inside those systems, under whose authority, and what happens when it is wrong.

We are a small provider group. Is this built for us?

Yes. This is built for provider groups and digital health teams, not hospital IT departments. The scope adjusts to your footprint.

What would an auditor find in your prompt logs today?

Most healthcare leaders don't know, and that's the risk. A 53-point score is a starting line, not a verdict. Let's find out where you stand.
